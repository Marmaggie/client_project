Moonbeam Concessions

No drive-in movie experience would be complete without classic movie snacks, and Moonbeam Concessions delivers all the favorites guests know and love. Located near the center of The RETRO Drive-In Theater, the concession stand offers quick and convenient service so guests can grab snacks and drinks without missing the movie. With glowing retro signs, classic theater aromas, and a nostalgic atmosphere, Moonbeam Concessions brings the traditional drive-in experience to life.

Guests can enjoy freshly popped buttery popcorn, candy, nachos, soft pretzels, frozen drinks, fountain beverages, and a variety of movie-night treats. Combo meals and family snack packs are also available for groups looking to share during the show. Whether it is a quick snack before the movie starts or a late-night refill during the second feature, Moonbeam Concessions is ready to keep guests fueled throughout the evening.
