Events

At The RETRO Drive-In Theater, there is always something exciting happening beyond the movies themselves. Guests can enjoy a variety of themed events throughout the season, including Classic Film Nights featuring timeless favorites from past decades, Family Nights with kid-friendly movies and activities, and Horror Nights packed with spooky films and late-night thrills. Special holiday events are also held during occasions like Halloween, Christmas, and summer weekends, often including themed decorations, music, contests, and exclusive food specials from the concession stand and restaurant. The RETRO also hosts community events such as car meetups, trivia nights, double-feature weekends, and outdoor live entertainment before select showings. Looking for a unique venue for your own celebration? Guests can rent one of the theater’s giant screens for birthdays, graduation parties, reunions, company events, and private movie nights, creating a memorable experience under the stars. With new events and themed nights added regularly, The RETRO offers more than just movies — it delivers a fun and nostalgic entertainment experience for all ages.

Event Info

Enjoy timeless movie classics on the last Saturday of every month. From black-and-white favorites to iconic retro films, Classic Film Night brings nostalgia to the big screen with themed snacks and vintage atmosphere.

Bring the whole family for fun, family-friendly movies every first Friday of the month. Guests can enjoy animated favorites, kid-friendly concessions, and a welcoming environment for all ages.

Get ready for thrills during Horror Night on the third Friday of every month. Featuring classic horror films, spooky decorations, and late-night showings, this event is perfect for horror fans.

Celebrate Memorial Day Weekend with an all-night movie marathon featuring action, comedy, and adventure films. Guests can enjoy special concession deals and double-feature screenings throughout the weekend.

Join us on July 4th for a patriotic movie night followed by a fireworks show. Guests can enjoy classic summer films, themed food specials, and a festive atmosphere under the stars.

Experience two nights of scares on October 30th and 31st with horror movie marathons, costume contests, and themed decorations throughout the theater grounds.

Celebrate the holiday season with classic Christmas movies from December 20th–23rd. Warm drinks, holiday treats, and festive lights make this one of our coziest events of the year.

Ring in the New Year with back-to-back movie showings on December 31st. Guests can enjoy special late-night snacks, countdown celebrations, and a memorable evening at The RETRO.
