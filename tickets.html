General  Admission


Admission to The RETRO Drive-In Theater includes access to double features on our large outdoor screens. Pricing is designed to be affordable for individuals, families, and groups.


Adults (13+): $10.00

Children (5–12): $6.00

Children (4 & under): Free

Seniors (60+): $8.00


Each ticket grants entry for one vehicle occupant. Prices may vary depending on special events or featured films.


Special  Event  Pricing


Special events at The RETRO Drive-In Theater may have adjusted admission prices depending on the event type, featured films, or live entertainment included with the showing. Events such as Horror Night, Classic Film Night, Family Night, holiday specials, and private community events may require advanced ticket purchases due to limited space and higher attendance. Pricing information for all special events will be posted in advance on the Events pages. Guests are encouraged to arrive early during special events, as parking spots may fill quickly.


Tickets


Tickets can be purchased at the theater entrance on the day of the showing or reserved ahead of time for select events and busy weekends. Online reservation options may be available for featured events and private rentals. Guests should keep their ticket confirmation or receipt available upon arrival. Admission is charged per person, and all guests entering the theater grounds must have a valid ticket unless otherwise stated for promotional events or children under the free admission age.



Payment  Methods  Accepted


The RETRO Drive-In Theater accepts several convenient payment methods to make the check-in and concession process quick and simple. Guests may pay using cash, major credit cards, debit cards, and select mobile payment options. Some special events, rentals, or online reservations may require card payment in advance. Exact payment methods accepted may vary depending on ticket location, event type, or temporary technical issues.


Parking  Information


Parking at The RETRO Drive-In Theater is available on a first-come, first-served basis. Guests are encouraged to arrive early to secure a preferred viewing spot and allow time for parking before the movie begins. Larger vehicles may be directed to designated areas to avoid blocking screen visibility for other guests. For the best viewing experience, guests should remain within their assigned parking space and follow all staff directions while entering and exiting the theater grounds. Radio instructions for movie audio will be provided upon arrival.
