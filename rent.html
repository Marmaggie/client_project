Want To Rent a Screen?

Make your event unforgettable at The RETRO Drive-In Theater by renting one of our large outdoor screens. Whether you're planning a birthday party, school function, corporate event, family reunion, or private movie night, our drive-in experience offers a unique way to celebrate under the stars. Guests can enjoy full access to our projection system, audio setup, and optional concession services to enhance the event. Our team will work with you to help create a smooth and memorable experience from start to finish.

Pricing and Polcies

Screen rental pricing varies depending on the size of the event, day of the week, film licensing requirements, and additional services requested such as concessions or restaurant access. All rentals require advance booking and approval to ensure availability and proper scheduling. A deposit may be required to secure your date, with final pricing confirmed after event details are reviewed. Guests are expected to follow all theater guidelines during private events to ensure safety, respect for property, and an enjoyable experience for everyone attending.
