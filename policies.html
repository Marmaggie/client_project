Policy

Outside Food and Beverage Policy


To help support The RETRO Drive-In Theater’s concession stand and restaurant services, outside food and beverages are discouraged unless approved by management. Guests are encouraged to enjoy the snacks, drinks, and meals available on-site during their visit. Small personal items such as bottled water or baby food are permitted. Any large coolers, catering trays, or outside deliveries may be restricted during events or busy showings.

Vehicle Speed and Safety Policy


For the safety of all guests, vehicles must remain at a slow speed while driving through the theater grounds. Drivers should follow all posted signs and staff directions when entering, parking, or exiting the property. Reckless driving, excessive speeding, or unsafe vehicle behavior may result in removal from the theater without refund. Guests should remain alert for pedestrians, especially children, in parking and walking areas.

Noise and Courtesy Policy


Guests are expected to be respectful of others during movie showings and events. Loud conversations, excessive music, disruptive behavior, or unnecessary vehicle movement may disturb nearby viewers and are not permitted. Radios should remain at a reasonable volume, and guests should silence phones whenever possible. Theater staff reserve the right to address disruptive behavior to maintain a positive experience for all visitors.

Pet Policy


Friendly and well-behaved pets are welcome at The RETRO Drive-In Theater as long as they remain supervised at all times. Pets must be kept on a leash when outside of vehicles and owners are responsible for cleaning up after them. Any pet showing aggressive or disruptive behavior may be asked to leave the property. Service animals are always permitted in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

Parking and Vehicle Placement Policy


Vehicles must park within designated spaces as directed by theater staff. Larger vehicles such as trucks, vans, and SUVs may be assigned to specific parking rows to prevent blocking the screen view for other guests. Guests may not reserve extra parking spaces or move theater equipment without permission. For safety reasons, vehicles should remain parked once the movie begins unless exiting the theater.

Weather and Cancellation Policy


Movies are shown rain or shine whenever possible; however, severe weather conditions may result in delays, cancellations, or early closures for guest safety. In the event of a cancellation before or during a showing, management may provide rain checks, ticket transfers, or other accommodations depending on the situation. Guests are encouraged to check the website or social media pages for weather-related updates before arrival.

Smoking and Alcohol Policy


Smoking, vaping, and the use of electronic cigarettes are only permitted in designated smoking areas away from crowded guest spaces. Illegal substances and underage alcohol consumption are strictly prohibited on theater property. Guests who appear excessively intoxicated or who violate these rules may be removed from the premises without refund. The RETRO strives to maintain a safe and family-friendly environment for all visitors.

Cleanliness and Trash Disposal Policy


Guests are asked to help keep the theater grounds clean by properly disposing of all trash and recycling before leaving. Trash containers are located throughout the property near parking areas, concession spaces, and walkways. Damaging property, littering, or leaving excessive messes behind may result in additional cleaning fees or loss of future admission privileges. Keeping the grounds clean helps maintain an enjoyable environment for everyone.

Children Supervision Policy


Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising children at all times while on theater property. Children should not run through parking areas, near vehicles, or around theater equipment without adult supervision. During movies and events, families are encouraged to remain mindful of surrounding guests and maintain a safe environment. Theater staff may intervene if unsafe behavior is observed.
