Starlight Diner

Step into the nostalgic atmosphere of the Starlight Diner, The RETRO Drive-In Theater’s classic movie-themed restaurant experience. Inspired by vintage American diners and late-night movie culture, the Starlight Diner offers guests a cozy place to relax before the show or enjoy a meal while watching films under the stars. Guests can enjoy outdoor seating with direct views of the theater screens, creating the perfect combination of dinner and entertainment.

The restaurant serves a variety of classic comfort foods including burgers, sandwiches, pizzas, appetizers, desserts, milkshakes, and refreshing drinks. Whether guests are stopping in for a quick snack or a full meal with family and friends, the Starlight Diner is designed to bring together great food, retro atmosphere, and the magic of outdoor movies.
